⚠️ Known limits of this backtest (read once)
• Uses daily closing prices only. The live rule "rising day + buy the dip" happens intraday — here every fill is at the day's close, so entries are approximate.
• Survivorship bias: only today's universe is tested.
• Slippage is a flat % cost per side, not real order-book depth.
• Volume filter uses bridge data when available; falls back to no-filter when volume unavailable.
• Dividends and tracking error are not modelled.

Source-of-truth build from the supplied Version 2.0 rules. The linked underlying index creates the weekly candle. GREEN buys only on a bullish index week; RED buys only on a bearish index week. BUY/BID signals are actionable only after 3:00 PM IST on the final NSE trading session of the Monday→Friday week. SELL signals remain active every trading day.

⚙ Tenet 2.0 Rule Settings
Hard contract: NEW pool 60% · BID pool 40% · one BID/week · largest eligible fall wins · no stop-loss · maximum 20 GREEN slots. Data safety is separate from strategy logic: Verified live is the default for real-money action buttons. The 52W percentage is a universe selection/removal guide, not an automatic live-signal veto in V2.0.
🧩 Tenet Scanner Universe
ETFs are created once in AlphaDesk's Master ETF Universe and linked to their real underlying index. Tenet then assigns a GREEN ETF and, when available, a separate RED counter ETF from the same underlying. GREEN is normally the highest-turnover ETF; RED is the next suitable counter. RED may be blank when only one ETF exists. Keep a pair while either ETF still has an open Tenet holding.
Tenet Homa 2.0 Backtest
Canonical simulation: Monday→Friday trading week; weekly entry at the final session CLOSE (the sheet's after-3PM approximation); daily target exits using the day's HIGH at the exact target price; strict LIFO; no stop-loss. Historical point-in-time universe snapshots are used when supplied; otherwise the result is labelled CONFIGURED-UNIVERSE. Partial final weeks are not treated as completed weekly signals.

Tax Rules Applied
Three tax tiers apply — asset class decides which:
• Equity ETFs (NIFTYBEES, BANKBEES, MOM100…) — STCG 20% · LTCG 12.5% with ₹1.25L exemption per FY. Both +4% cess.
• Gold & International ETFs (GOLDBEES, SILVERBEES, MON100, MAFANG…) — not equity-oriented, so §111A and §112A don't apply: STCG at your slab rate · LTCG 12.5% with NO ₹1.25L exemption. Both +4% cess.
• Debt ETFs (LIQUIDBEES…) — slab rate always, no LTCG benefit, no exemption. +4% cess.
• Holding period — 12 calendar months for all listed ETFs (not 365 days)
• Loss offset — losses within FY offset gains before tax calculation
• Carryforward — net loss carried forward to offset future FY gains (up to 8 years)
